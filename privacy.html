ClipBot is operated by [YOUR NAME]. This policy explains what data ClipBot uses.

1. Data from your TikTok account

When you log in with TikTok, ClipBot receives basic profile information (user.info.basic) and permission to publish videos you choose to post (video.publish). The access and refresh tokens are stored only on your own computer. We do not keep them on our servers.

2. Stream content

Live stream video and audio are recorded and processed on your own computer to create clips. To suggest a category, title and hashtags, short text excerpts (a speech transcript of the clip and recent public chat messages) are sent to the Anthropic API for analysis. Video files are not sent.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your personal data.
  • We do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies on this website.

4. Retention and control

Clips and tokens remain on your computer until you delete them. You can revoke ClipBot's access at any time in your TikTok account settings (connected apps) and delete the local files.

5. Children
