ClipBot

ClipBot is a tool that helps creators turn highlights from live streams into short vertical videos and publish them to their own TikTok account.

How it works

  1. The creator chooses a live stream they have permission to clip.
  2. ClipBot detects exciting moments (louder audio, chat activity) and cuts a short clip.
  3. The clip is formatted vertically (9:16) with a title and hashtags suggested automatically.
  4. After the creator connects their TikTok account with TikTok Login, ClipBot publishes the clips to that same account.
